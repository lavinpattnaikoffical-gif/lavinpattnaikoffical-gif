## 👋 About Me

I'm **Lavin Pattnaik** — an **AI & IoT Innovator** and **Full-Stack Developer** who lives at the intersection of computer vision, intelligent systems, and real-world hardware. I build things that make machines see, understand, and respond to the world around them.

My work spans from gesture-controlled interfaces powered by computer vision to self-hosted AI chat agents running entirely on local hardware. I believe in privacy-first, open-source technology — tools that put power back in the hands of the builder.

When I'm not shipping projects, I'm exploring the bleeding edge of AI, experimenting with new paradigms in human-computer interaction, and pushing what's possible with Python, TypeScript, and a webcam.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🎨 [Air Canvas](https://github.com/lavinpattnaikoffical-gif/Air-Canvas)
> *Turn your webcam into an invisible canvas. Draw in the air with your fingers.*

A real-time computer vision application that tracks hand landmarks using **MediaPipe** and **OpenCV** to let you paint on a digital screen — no stylus, no touchscreen, just your hand. Features gesture-based drawing, erasing, hovering, and full-canvas clearing with a built-in jitter stabilizer for smooth curves.

`Python` · `OpenCV` · `MediaPipe` · `NumPy` · ⭐ 3 stars · 🍴 2 forks

---

### ✋ [Hand Gesture Control System](https://github.com/lavinpattnaikoffical-gif/Hand-Gesture-Control-System)
> *Control your entire system — volume, brightness, media — with nothing but your hands.*

A Python application that uses webcam input and hand landmark detection to give you gesture-based control over system functions. Think of it as the remote control you never have to pick up.

`Python` · `Computer Vision` · `MediaPipe` · ⭐ 2 stars · 🍴 1 fork

---

### 🤖 [SYNERGY AI — Local AI Chat Agent](https://github.com/lavinpattnaikoffical-gif/SYNERGY-AI---Local-AI-Chat-Agent)
> *A modern ChatGPT-style interface for local LLMs — 100% local, 100% private.*

A full-stack AI chat platform built on **Next.js** and **Ollama** that runs entirely on your machine. Features streaming responses, conversation persistence, file analysis (PDF, TXT, MD), syntax-highlighted code blocks, and a self-hosted **WhatsApp bot** — no cloud APIs, no data leaks.

`TypeScript` · `Next.js` · `React` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Ollama`

---

### 🛡️ [Scam Honeypot API](https://github.com/lavinpattnaikoffical-gif/Scam-Honeypot-API)
